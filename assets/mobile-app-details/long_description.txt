🃏 THE SUPREME DADAJI JOKER
The deck features 52 cards where the 2 of Spades has been permanently removed to crown the legendary Dadaji Joker!
• In Tricks 2–13, Dadaji reigns supreme, outranking even the Ace of Trump.
• You can unleash Dadaji even when holding cards in the led suit!
• If led, Dadaji counts as the 2 of Spades.
• Beware: On the 13th and final trick, Dadaji is devalued to a weak card, penalizing greedy players who hold him too long!

🎨 13th CARD TRUMP COLOR
Before each hand begins, the dealer reveals the 13th card from the deck, establishing the round's Trump Color. If Dadaji appears as the 13th card, an intense "Universal Joker" no-trump round is triggered!

⚖️ STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP BIDDING
• Inspect your hand and place your bids from 0 to 13 tricks.
• Each partnership must bid a combined minimum of 2 tricks.
• 6-Bid Rule: If the sum of bids between both teams is less than 6, a reshuffle is called to guarantee competitive, thrilling gameplay.
• High-Risk, High-Reward: Bidding 10+ tricks earns double points (+20 bonus), while capturing all 13 tricks achieves the legendary Boston Sweep (+26 points)!

⚡ SMART SQUEEZABLE ACTION BANNERS
• "Skip Remaining Tricks": Once both team contracts are secured, save time by cleanly claiming the remaining rounds.
• "Show Hand": Confident in your unlosable cards? Call "Show Hand" to reveal your winning cards and instantly claim contract victory!
